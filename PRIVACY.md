# FocusFlow — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026 (v1.1.0)_

FocusFlow is a Pomodoro timer and website blocker published by Kavara Digital.

## What stays on your device
Your timer state, settings, blocklists, focus statistics (daily focus time, sessions,
blocked-site counts, XP and badges) and any uploaded focus sound are stored in your
browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage`). Settings and
blocklists may sync across your own signed-in browsers through your browser's built-in
sync. Kavara Digital never receives this data.

## Website access
FocusFlow runs a script on the pages you visit so it can show a "blocked" screen on
sites in your blocklist during a focus session. It checks only the page's address
against your blocklist. Browsing activity is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

## Feedback
If you choose to send feedback, the following is sent to support@kavaradigital.com
through the FormSubmit email service (formsubmit.co): your message, feedback type,
optional star rating, the email address you enter (optional), the FocusFlow version,
and your browser name and user-agent string. We use this only to respond to you and
improve FocusFlow, and do not sell or share it.

## Focus sounds from a URL or YouTube
Chimes are generated on your device. If you choose an audio URL as your focus sound,
FocusFlow streams that file from the address you entered while the timer runs. If you
choose a YouTube video, FocusFlow streams it from youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube's
privacy-enhanced embed) while the timer runs, and shows its thumbnail from i.ytimg.com
in Settings. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback.
